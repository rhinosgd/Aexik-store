# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Formula 1 fans in Europe, the United States, Canada, Australia, and Japan who want a desk setup or a gift that carries genuine racing culture beyond generic car imagery. They compare desk mats, mouse pads, and low-cost fan accessories, then expect clear sizes, finishes, shipping information, and a meaningful product story.

## Product Purpose

APEXIK sells collectible, made-to-order desk mats, mouse pads, and keychains that transform Formula 1 race strategy, team-radio exchanges, controversial calls, and defining moments into usable designed objects. The immediate business goal is to attract Formula 1 fans with desk surfaces, then earn add-on purchases and establish a base for future novelty and customized products.

## Positioning

Each APEXIK surface interprets a complete Formula 1 moment as an original visual record: strategic context, timing, radio, incident, and outcome become a designed composition rather than a quote printed as text.

## Operating Context

Shoppers browse on mobile and desktop before or after a race weekend, compare desk dimensions and edge finishes, and may add a keychain as a connected collectible. WooCommerce variable products use Size and Style selections. The site needs a homepage, collection pages, product pages, and an archive of race stories that links to relevant products.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- WordPress and WooCommerce with an existing custom theme, starter-content plugin, and SEO/GEO plugin.
- Preserve the existing information architecture where practical; focus the product mix on desk mats, mouse pads, and keychains first.
- Desk mats and mouse pads must offer Size and Style variations; the starter desk mat uses 800 x 300 mm, 900 x 400 mm, and 1000 x 500 mm with Standard Edge and Stitched Edge.
- All material purchase facts, prices, shipping claims, stock, and manufacturing claims remain subject to supplier confirmation.

## Brand Commitments

- Brand: APEXIK.
- Display typography: Formula1-Bold for title-level type.
- The brand has confirmed authorization for F1 / Formula 1 names, race and results information, driver and team names, logos and liveries, team-radio wording, and race footage or photography.
- Use the confirmed rights truthfully and retain per-asset rights records in the existing admin workflow.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing WordPress theme and plugins under `wordpress/wp-content/`.
- Current product images and homepage assets in the theme and starter plugin.
- Competitor research: Etsy shows dense desk-mat competition and strong demand for team-radio keychains; APEXIK must avoid generic car imagery and compete through race-moment design systems.
- No supplier-verified cost, product photography, fulfillment lead time, pricing, customer review, or shipping-policy evidence is available yet.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with a race moment and a usable product in the same frame.
2. Make every design readable as an archive, not a slogan.
3. Let desk mats create consideration; let keychains create add-on momentum.
4. Use factual, crawlable content to make the product, its fit, and its story understandable to shoppers and search systems.
5. Do not invent commercial proof or product specifications that are not confirmed.
