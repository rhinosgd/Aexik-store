# APEXIK Design Direction

## Direction

Race Control Archive / Archive Desk with Night Trace motion.

APEXIK treats Formula 1 moments as source material for useful desk objects. The interface should feel like a race-control archive opened on a working desk: precise, editorial, slightly tense, and product-led.

## Tokens

- Display: self-hosted `Formula1-Bold` for titles, product names, labels, and navigation emphasis.
- Body: `IBM Plex Sans` with Arial fallback for readable product and purchase copy.
- Paper: `#f4f0e8`.
- Ink: `#10100f` / `#151514`.
- Signal red: `#c51d24`.
- Supporting neutrals: bone text on dark surfaces and graphite text on paper surfaces.
- Shapes: mostly square edges with restrained 3px radius; borders are 1px technical rules.

## Homepage

- Hero: one desk-mat product image, one red route trace, one primary shop action.
- Product hierarchy: Desk Mats, Mouse Pads, and Keychains appear before the brand story.
- Archive story: text explains that each design combines the moment, strategy, radio, and aftermath rather than printing a plain quote.
- Guides: size, gifting, and Team Radio collection links make the commercial surface crawlable and useful.

## Product Purchase

- Desk mats and mouse pads use WooCommerce variable products with `Size` and `Style` attributes.
- The purchase area explicitly labels both choices and links to the surface size guide.
- Surface products use functional proof points: tracking surface, stable base, and edge choice.

## Motion and Accessibility

- The red trace is the single authored homepage moment and is disabled for reduced-motion users.
- Focus rings remain visible on controls and collection links.
- Images carry descriptive alt text; decorative trace geometry is hidden from assistive technology.
- The mobile composition stacks the archive, surface products, story record, and keychain add-on without horizontal scrolling.

## Content Rules

- Use authorized Formula 1 references only within the store's approved rights scope.
- Keep product facts separate from story copy; supplier-dependent material, dispatch, and compatibility claims require verification.
- Use search language naturally: Formula 1 desk mats, motorsport mouse pads, team radio gifts, racing desk setup, and race fan keychains.
